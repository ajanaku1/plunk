My read. Peek is the strongest at 16 pixels and is the only one that works as a mascot on the box page. Pop is the cleanest as a pure logo and tells the "one price" story best. Drop is the most elegant at large sizes but at 16 pixels it starts to look like a lighter or a candle. Green is one step off NEAR green and purple one step off Solana purple, so the marks read as Plunk rather than as either chain.
