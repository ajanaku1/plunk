# Plunk — art direction (draft for Checkpoint A, 2026-11-06)

## Brand truth (draft; becomes BRAND-TRUTH.md after the mark is picked)
- **name:** Plunk ($PLUNK)
- **purpose:** a NEAR meme launchpad where everyone bids into a sealed box and it pops open at one fair price, and a dev who sells early "gets plunked" by the dev tax.
- **core_verb:** plunk (drop NEAR into the box)
- **metaphor:** a sealed box that swallows bids and pops open at one level price; nobody sees inside until it opens.
- **palette (proposed):** one accent, Plunk Tangerine `#FF5A1F`; ink `#14110F`; paper `#F6F2EA`; mid-grey `#8C857B`.
- **constraints:** must read at 16 px (favicon) and as a mascot in the box-page states (Closed, Reveal, Opening, Open); flat vector; no text inside the mark.

## Signature element: "the Lid"
A plain square box whose lid floats a little above it. The gap between lid and box is the only "window"; two round eyes peek out of it.
- The **lid is a flat bar**: when the box opens it is the one clearing price, a single level line.
- The **gap** is the sealed-bid idea: you see that something is inside, never what.
- The same shape is the **mascot**. In the UI it acts out the box states: lid shut and eyes closed (Closed), eyes darting in the gap (Reveal), lid lifting (Opening), lid flipped up into the price line (Open). "Got plunked" is the lid slamming down on a dev.

## Rejected literal ideas (forbidden)
Piggy bank, coin slot, treasure chest, gift box with ribbon, rocket or moon, meme animals (dogs, frogs, cats), candles or charts, a coin with a ticker on it, a hexagon or a cube with nodes, the NEAR logo or NEAR green.

## Material and composition
Flat vector, thick even strokes or solid shapes, rounded corners on one radius, centred with generous space, high-contrast silhouette. Tangerine is the only colour; everything else is ink or paper.

## The three marks to draw (Stage 3)
1. **Peek** — box plus floating lid, two eyes in the gap.
2. **Drop** — box with a single drop falling into the gap.
3. **Pop** — lid flipped up and flattened into a level bar above the open box.

## Round 2 (2026-11-06): palette change and polish

- User chose **Peek** as the mark and changed the palette to green / purple / black
  (NEAR green, Solana purple). Tangerine is dropped.
- Working palette: green `#2EE89C`, purple `#9B6BFF`, black `#0E0B14`, light `#F3F0FA`.
  Each hue sits one step off the exact NEAR / Solana brand values on purpose.
- Image-model route (free Pollinations/Flux) was tried for a "real product" look:
  3 of 4 seeds failed, the one result was an unrelated glowing cube with a watermark.
  Paid image MCPs were not connected. Polish was done as crafted vector instead:
  `marks/peek-icon.svg` (flat depth), `marks/peek-iso.svg` (3D box), `marks/peek-light.svg`.
